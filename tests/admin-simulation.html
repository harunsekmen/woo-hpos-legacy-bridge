Bridge Diagnostics

With WooCommerce HPOS enabled, older extensions (shipping labels, invoice generators, ERP syncs) that call get_post_meta($order_id) fail silently because orders no longer reside in wp_posts.

This bridge intercepts the WordPress get_post_metadata filter and seamlessly routes reads and writes directly to $order->get_meta() and $order->update_meta_data().

🛡️ Safe Migration Promise: Enable HPOS with 100% confidence. No need to rewrite hundreds of custom legacy functions.
